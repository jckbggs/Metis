Welcome to Metis

Your student support chatbot for assignment brief information, assignment calendars, and mitigating circumstances advice.

How it works

Metis helps students quickly find useful academic support information. After logging in, you can choose one of the specialist bots depending on what you need help with.

  • Assignment Brief Information – understand requirements, tasks, and submission expectations.
  • Assignment Calendars – check deadlines, upcoming dates, and schedules.
  • Mitigating Circumstances Advice – get guidance on eligibility, evidence, and application steps.
