Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using Metis and accessing student support information.

General Questions

What is Metis?

Metis is a student support platform that helps users access academic information, including assignment brief guidance, assignment calendars, mitigating circumstances advice, and assignment review support.

Do I need to log in to use Metis?

Some features may be available without logging in, but logging in allows you to access personalised tools and services more easily.

Who is Metis for?

Metis is designed for students who need quick and clear access to academic support, deadlines, and university guidance.

Assignment Support

What can the Assignment Brief Information bot do?

It can help explain assignment requirements, clarify tasks, and point you toward important details about expectations and submissions.

What does the Assignment Calendar tool do?

It helps you organise deadlines, track important academic dates, and plan your assignment schedule more effectively.

Can Metis review my assignment?

Metis can provide support through the Assignment Reviewer tool, which is designed to help you reflect on your work before submission.

Mitigating Circumstances

What are mitigating circumstances?

Mitigating circumstances are serious, unexpected situations that may have affected your ability to complete an assessment or meet a deadline.

Can Metis submit a mitigating circumstances claim for me?

No. Metis provides guidance and information, but you should follow your institution’s official process to submit a claim.

What kind of evidence might be needed?

Evidence depends on the situation and your institution’s rules. Metis can provide general guidance, but you should always check your official university policy.

Technical Support

What should I do if a page is not loading?

Try refreshing the page first. If the issue continues, contact the support team using the Contact Us page.

What should I do if I cannot log in?

Double-check your email and password. If you still cannot access your account, use the Contact Us page for further help.
