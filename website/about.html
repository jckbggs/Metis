About Metis

Metis is a student support platform designed to help students easily access important academic information, guidance, and planning tools in one place.

Our Purpose

Metis was created to simplify access to academic support. Many students struggle to find clear information about assignment briefs, deadlines, and mitigating circumstances. Metis brings this information together into one easy-to-use system.

Our goal is to reduce confusion, save time, and help students feel more confident when managing their assignments and academic responsibilities.

What Metis Helps With

  • Assignment Brief Information – explains assignment requirements and expectations.
  • Assignment Calendars – helps track deadlines and plan your time.
  • Mitigating Circumstances Advice – explains the process and what evidence you may need.
  • Assignment Review Support – helps you review your work before submission.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make academic support more accessible, clearer, and easier to use. Metis is designed to guide students to the right information quickly, without needing to search through multiple websites or documents.
