Learning Outcomes Aligned to this Assessment
Aim:
This assessment aims to develop and demonstrate knowledge and understanding of conducting requirements engineering, designing, and documenting system specifications and interfaces for a client-driven project based on simulated client interviews and collaborative teamwork.

Subject-Specific Knowledge, Understanding and Application:
a) Gather and interpret client requirements.
Identify and understand critical information from client interviews to capture and define system needs.

b) Analyse and document findings.
Record and interpret findings from elicitation activities, highlighting essential system requirements and constraints.

c) Develop comprehensive system specifications.
Create detailed requirement specifications and interface designs based on findings from client interactions and further research.

d) Design system architecture.
Produce system design documentation that includes wireframes, mock-ups, navigation diagrams, class analysis documents and architectural designs to support the subsequent effective development of the proposed solution.

e) Evaluate project documentation quality.
Review and refine project reports to ensure completeness, coherence, and adherence to professional standards.

Background
Client: Claybrook Zoo
Location: Northwest England
Established: 1965
Current Zoo Manager: Matthew Jones

Claybrook Zoo is a small, family-oriented zoo with a long history of supporting educational initiatives to enhance visitor experiences. The zoo has approached your software development team to investigate the possibility of developing a mobile application that will enhance the experience of visitors during their time at the zoo.

Your task is to develop the specifications, interface designs, and architectural blueprints for this mobile application. The initial client requirements are listed below and include enough information for you to develop a simple solution without needing to interview the client.

Initial System Requirements
The zoo houses animals across several distinct areas:
- Aviary (Birds)
- Hothouse (Reptiles and Amphibians)
- Aquarium (Fish)
- Cages/Compounds (Mammals)

The mobile application must provide interactive visitor engagement features, including:
- Interactive Zoo Map: a digital map displaying the zoo layout, with real-time navigation to different enclosures, attractions, and amenities.
- Animal Information Portal: visitors can scan QR codes placed at each enclosure to access detailed information about animals, including species name, given name, date of birth, gender, average life span, category/classification, dietary requirements, natural habitat, global population distribution, conservation status, fun facts, and audio clips.
- Personalised Tour Guide: visitors can create custom itineraries based on their interests such as species, feeding times, or special events.
- Augmented Reality features: 3D visualisations of animals and habitats when visitors scan designated markers.
- Live Zoo Updates and Notifications: real-time updates on feeding times, talks, events, and promotions.
- Visitor Feedback System: visitors can rate exhibits and suggest improvements.
- Multi-Language Support: multilingual translations of content.

Core Application Features
The mobile application must provide the following key functionalities:
1. QR Code Scanning
2. GPS-Based Navigation
3. Customisable Alerts and Reminders
4. Visitor Engagement Tools
   - Photo and video sharing with social media integration
   - Interactive quizzes and scavenger hunts
5. Zoo Membership and Ticketing Integration
   - Ticket and membership purchasing
   - Digital passes for contactless entry
6. Accessibility Features
   - Audio descriptions for visually impaired visitors
   - Sign language video guides for key exhibits

Additional Features / Complex Functionality
- Animal Adoption Programme
- Live Camera Feeds
- Educational Content and Games
- Push Notifications
- Virtual Tour Mode
- Zoo Merchandise Store
